# Competitor branding review — 2026-11-23

Quick brand scan of seven conversational / voice AI companies, for RaiokiuAI inspiration.

**Method.** Each homepage was loaded in a browser on 2026-11-23. The headline, `<title>`, meta description, fonts, button colours and JSON-LD were read from the rendered page. Colours are the computed values of the main call-to-action button. This was a single snapshot, so nothing here can show whether something has changed over time.

**Not covered.** No SEO data source was connected, so traffic, keyword and backlink metrics were not collected. Logos and imagery are described from screenshots, not from brand files.

Each section separates **observed** facts from **inferred** readings and **implications** for RaiokiuAI.

## Patterns (inferred)

1. **Violet and blue are the default colours of Indian conversational AI.** Gupshup (violet `#5956D6`), Haptik (blue/violet gradient) and Verloop (blue on navy) all sit in the same hue band as RaiokiuAI's `#4B25FB`. Medium confidence: this is based on homepages only. Yellow.ai (yellow) and Sarvam (orange/black) stand out partly because they avoid it.
2. **Almost everyone puts "AI agents" in the H1.** Five of seven use the literal phrase "AI agents" or "voice agents" in the headline. RaiokiuAI's H1 says "Agents" but not "AI agents".
3. **Nearly every competitor offers a live demo of the product.** Yellow (chat), Verloop (phone), Vapi (browser voice call) and Gupshup (chat) let a visitor try the agent before talking to sales. RaiokiuAI's only CTA is a form.
4. **Proof comes before features.** Every site shows a number, a logo strip, a review count or a testimonial in the first screen.
5. **Differentiated brands use type, not just colour.** Sarvam (soft display) and Verloop (serif), and to a lesser degree Retell (thin condensed) and Vapi (Avantt), look different mainly because of their display typeface. The others use generic grotesks.
6. **Most position on "agents"; nobody leads with "workflows".** Yellow mentions "Act", but "think, act, resolve" describes the agent, not a workflow product. RaiokiuAI's "Workflows that act" half is unclaimed territory among these seven.
7. **Sarvam owns "from India".** "AI for all from India" and "India's Full-Stack Sovereign AI Platform" make India the brand. RaiokiuAI mentions India only in the footer.

## Implications and options for RaiokiuAI

These are options to weigh, not conclusions.

### Brand

- **Lean into "workflows that act".** This is the most ownable idea we have. Consider making the branching node in the logo (one input splitting into two outputs) the visual motif across the site: bullets, dividers, diagrams and loading states. Haptik does this with its hand mark.
- **Differentiate from the violet crowd without abandoning violet.** Options:
  - (a) Keep electric violet but add one signature secondary colour no competitor uses, such as lime, teal or warm coral, for highlights and data.
  - (b) Make the "chrome" lavender gradient (`--chrome`) the recognisable element instead of flat violet.
  - (c) Stay dark-first, which already separates us from Gupshup's light, gradient-heavy look.
- **Typography is already a strength.** Bricolage Grotesque is more distinctive than Manrope, Proxima Nova or Founders Grotesk. Keep it, and consider using its wider optical sizes for headlines so the difference is more visible.
- **Decide the India story.** Either make it a feature (Indian languages, amounts read as lakh/crore, WhatsApp-first) without competing with Sarvam's "sovereign AI" framing, or keep it secondary. Today it's in the footer and in passing copy.

### Conversion and proof

- **Add a live demo agent.** This is the strongest pattern observed. Options: "Message our agent on WhatsApp", a web chat widget built on our own SDK, or "Call our voice agent". It is also the best proof that the product works.
- **Proof that doesn't need customer logos.** While in early access, options include:
  - Concrete, supportable numbers: languages supported (the channels page shows 8), channels, and models behind the gateway.
  - A "design partners" programme.
  - A founder note.
  - A short product walkthrough video.
- Don't use model-provider logos as "trusted by" badges. They are integrations, not customers.

### SEO

- **Put "AI agents" in the home H1 or directly under it.** For example, a small eyebrow above the headline: "AI agent platform for WhatsApp, web & voice".
- **Use numbers in meta descriptions** where we can back them up, such as "8 languages" or "4 channels". Competitors do this consistently.
- **Comparison pages** (the `competitors` skill): "RaiokiuAI vs Yellow.ai", "Gupshup alternatives" and so on. Each page needs fair, sourced claims, and every gap should be confirmed in the competitor's docs before publishing.
- **Retell's FAQPage + SoftwareApplication pattern** is worth copying once we have public pricing and reviews. Until then, `Service` is the right choice (see README).
- **More `sameAs` profiles** (GitHub, YouTube, Crunchbase, Product Hunt) once they exist. Retell lists 8.
